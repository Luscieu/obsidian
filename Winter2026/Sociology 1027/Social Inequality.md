- Equality: equal opportunity and status
- Equity: being fair through remedying social oppression to rise disadvantaged up to the same level as those who are not
- Social Scientists rarely speak in absolutes
- Relative age advantage: age groups sperated by year, but within the year not all are equal, those born earlier have longer time to amass experience or grow

Selection - social factors that allows one to experience something. ex. family status
Streaming - when people are placed in social environments that magnify these advantages or disadvantages. ex. free education quality
Differentiated experiences - unequal outcomes a results of above. ex. university admission

Social Inequality: Condition and process in which preferential access to the goods are not randomly distributed but patterned around human differences that are defined as socially significant
