Man as political animal
- those who can speak through language, not just make noise
- politics an inescapable force in life, like tax and death
- shapes interests and conflict

Nation-state: a political unit claiming authority over a defined territory and population (a nation)
- Nation-states recognize each other as similar but ruling over another nation and state
- Realistically more nations than states
	- nations in same state fight over power to rule the state as a nation-state

Authoritarian systems:
- monarchies and dictatorships
- power concentrated to a few people
- few constraints on state power
	- those in power can pass laws with little thought

Totalitarian
- seek complete control over all aspects of life
- extreme control through use of force

Liberal Democracies
- rule based state
- constitutionalism and rule of law to divide power
- protects individual freedoms and rights
- free and fair elections
	- when elections are not free and fair they become illiberal democracies

Non-western views
- Worldism: Multi views
